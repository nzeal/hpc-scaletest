HPC-ScaleTest Documentation

Welcome to HPC-ScaleTest’s comprehensive documentation!

HPC-ScaleTest is an automated framework for generating and executing scaling studies on HPC systems. It simplifies the process of testing how well your application performs as you increase computing resources.
